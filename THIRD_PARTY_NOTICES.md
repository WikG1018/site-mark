# Third-party notices

SiteMark includes open-source dependencies distributed through Flutter pub and
Rust crates.io. Their license metadata is recorded in `pubspec.lock` and
`rust/Cargo.lock`.

## Noto Sans CJK SC

The bundled `rust/assets/fonts/NotoSansSC-Regular.otf` is part of Noto Sans CJK
and is used to render Simplified Chinese and Latin watermark text consistently.

Copyright 2014-2026 Adobe and Google. The font is licensed under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1. The complete license is included at
`rust/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk

## MiSans

The bundled `assets/fonts/MiSans-Regular.ttf` and
`assets/fonts/MiSans-Medium.ttf` are subsets of MiSans (Xiaomi HyperOS system
typeface), used as the app UI face.

- Typeface copyright © Xiaomi.
- Free for commercial use; embedding in a distributed app is allowed.
- The software must note that it uses MiSans (see the About screen).

The complete license PDF is included at `assets/fonts/LICENSE-MiSans.pdf`.

Source: https://hyperos.mi.com/font
